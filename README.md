✅ Topic Modeling (LDA) – Finds hidden themes & context in your text

🧠 Why LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation)?

🔹 Uncovers Hidden Topics – Groups related words into themes automatically
🔹 Scalable & Powerful – Works with wide, diverse datasets
🔹 Contextual Understanding – Goes beyond surface-level word matching
🔹 Best for Text Data – Optimized for natural language

💡 Example: A movie review dataset analyzed with LDA might reveal topics like actors, storyline, visuals, or ratings without any manual labeling.
